## 🎯 Overview

This repository serves as my **learning log and professional portfolio** for the UChicago MSADS program. It houses assignments, capstone work, and independent projects that demonstrate my progression in:

- **Data Engineering & Pipelines**: Building scalable ETL processes and working with cloud-native tools.
- **Statistical Modeling & Machine Learning**: Applying regression, classification, and deep learning techniques to real-world datasets.
- **Leadership & Consulting**: Translating data insights into actionable business strategies.
- **Reproducibility & Communication**: Writing clean, documented code and sharing findings through reports and visualizations.

The goal is to maintain a transparent, organized record of my academic journey and provide evidence of my technical and analytical capabilities to potential employers and collaborators.
