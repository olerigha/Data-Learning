# OODA Loop
**In one line:** Decisions run in a loop (Observe → Orient → Decide → Act), and whoever cycles through it faster wins.

**In plain words:** Data science teams are strong at the first half: observing data and orienting (making sense of it). Business decision-makers own the second half: deciding and acting. The gap between the two halves is where value gets lost. Too much data, black-box models, gut-feel leadership and weak business communication all erode trust. The fix comes from both sides: data scientists get closer to the business, and leaders build quantitative skills and bring the data team into decisions.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Observe | gather data and signals |
| Orient | interpret them in context |
| Decide | choose a course of action |
| Act | carry it out, then observe again |
| "Golden gut" | leaders trusting instinct over data |

**Key points**
- Data science team is highly involved in Observe/Orient; decision-makers in Decide/Act. Mind the gap.
- Wavestone 2026 survey: 93% of obstacles to data-driven transformation are **human** (culture, change management); only 7% are technical.
- **Data science side:** improve core skills, build non-quant skills, get involved in the business.
- **Decision-maker side:** develop quantitative skills, trust the data, include the data team.
- Problem-solving evaluation restarts observation, so the loop continues.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize O-O-D-A and the 93% / 7% finding.
