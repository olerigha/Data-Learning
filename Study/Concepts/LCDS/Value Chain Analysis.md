# Value Chain Analysis
**In one line:** Break a business into its activities to see where value is created and where costs can be cut. It's an internal view.

**In plain words:** Lay out the steps a company goes through to deliver its product: getting supplies in, making the product, shipping it, selling it, servicing it. Plus the support activities behind them. Then ask, step by step, where the bottlenecks, costs and opportunities are. For a data scientist, each link suggests analytics use cases.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Value chain | the sequence of activities that create value |
| Primary activities | inbound logistics → operations → outbound logistics → marketing & sales → service |
| Support activities | the functions behind them (HR, technology, procurement…) |
| Internal view | looks inside the company (vs Five Forces, which looks at the industry) |

**Key points**
- Purpose: find where value is created and where costs can be reduced.
- Example (retail): logistics and supply chain, merchandising, store operations, marketing, customer experience. Each maps to analytics: supplier performance, marketing-mix optimization, promotion effectiveness, customer lifetime value, loyalty, labor optimization.
- Manufacturer example: map logistics → operations → outbound → marketing → service to spot bottlenecks.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the primary activities and "internal view".
