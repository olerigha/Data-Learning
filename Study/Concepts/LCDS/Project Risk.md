# Project Risk
**In one line:** List what could go wrong early, give each risk an owner and a fix in a risk register, and review it at every status meeting.

**In plain words:** Every project has the usual dangers: scope creep, people not available, running over time or budget, vendors being late. Data science adds its own: the data is missing or dirty, the model can't hit its accuracy target, bias or privacy problems, the model drifts after launch, experiments don't pan out, one key person leaves, or people trust the model too much. Naming them early lets you plan around them.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Risk register | list of risks, each with an owner and a mitigation |
| Mitigation | action that lowers a risk's chance or impact |
| Scope creep | the work quietly growing beyond what was agreed |
| Model drift | model performance degrades as real-world data changes |
| Key-person risk | depending on one scarce person or skill |
| Overtrust | users accept model outputs without enough scrutiny |

**Key points**
- **Traditional risks:** scope creep, resource availability, schedule and budget overruns, vendor and dependency delays.
- **AI / data science risks:** missing or dirty data, model misses its target, bias / privacy / compliance, drift after deployment, uncertain experiments, key-person risk, overtrust.
- Revisit the register at every status meeting.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the AI-specific risks.
