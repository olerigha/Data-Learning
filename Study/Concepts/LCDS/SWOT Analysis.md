# SWOT Analysis
**In one line:** A 2×2 that sets a company's internal Strengths and Weaknesses against external Opportunities and Threats.

**In plain words:** Two questions, two directions. Inside the company: what are we good at, and where are we weak? Outside: what's opening up, and what could hurt us? Putting them side by side shows strategic options. Use strengths to grab opportunities, fix weaknesses that threats would expose, and decide what to prioritize.

**Terms**
| | Positive | Negative |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal** | Strengths | Weaknesses |
| **External** | Opportunities | Threats |

**Key points**
- Purpose: generate strategic options and prioritize actions.
- Example: a small business uses its loyal customers and strong brand while addressing new competitors and regulatory changes.
- Other frameworks in Chapter 2: Business Model Canvas, 3 C's (Company, Customer, Competition), McKinsey 7-S, profitability (revenue − cost), supply vs demand, and profit driver trees (DuPont's ROI tree).
