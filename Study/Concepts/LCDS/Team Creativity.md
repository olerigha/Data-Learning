# Team Creativity
**In one line:** Creativity means ideas that are both new *and* useful. Teams need divergent and convergent thinking, the right conditions, and to avoid the things that kill it.

**In plain words:** A novel idea that doesn't solve the problem isn't creative in this sense. Teams first spread out to generate lots of ideas (divergent), then focus on the best (convergent). It takes domain skill, motivation, and the habit of seeing things from new angles and holding back judgment. Criticism, pressure to conform, dominant voices and fear of looking foolish shut it down. Diversity plus enough trust to speak freely opens it up.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Divergent thinking | generating many possible ideas |
| Convergent thinking | analyzing and narrowing to a solution |
| Cognitive interference | losing your train of thought while waiting to speak |
| Social inhibition | anxiety about being judged |
| Social loafing | less effort because others will carry it |
| Perspective taking | deliberately seeing it the way someone else would |

**Key points**
- **Needs:** domain-relevant skills, task motivation, flexible thinking (break mental sets, appreciate complexity, suspend judgment, use broad categories).
- **Hampered by:** negative climate, conformity and dominant members, interpersonal conflict, cognitive interference, inefficiency (teams are slower than individuals), social inhibition and loafing, and teams that are too stable or total strangers.
- **Enhanced by:** diversity (experience, culture, approach), some familiarity and trust, perspective taking.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize "new and useful" and divergent vs convergent.
