# Self-Awareness Loop
**In one line:** Notice your reaction, check your assumptions, then adapt how you respond, especially under pressure.

**In plain words:** Self-awareness isn't just knowing your personality type. It's seeing how your behavior lands on other people. Every strength has an overused version (confidence → dominance, analysis → overthinking, urgency → impatience). Learn your triggers. In the moment, pause: what am I assuming, and what does this person actually need? Then adjust your tone, pace or level of detail. Afterwards, reflect on what worked.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Notice | spot your first reaction: assumptions, emotion, tone, urge to respond |
| Check | "Am I reacting to the issue or how it was delivered? What do they need?" |
| Adapt | deliberately change tone, channel, detail or pace |
| Overused strength | a strength that turns into a weakness when pushed too far |
| PrinciplesYou | personality survey used in class to find your "archetype" |
| Emotional intelligence | self-awareness, empathy, confidence, conflict management, resilience |

**Key points**
- Know four things: your strengths, your overused strengths, your triggers, and your impact (intent ≠ how others experience you).
- 52% of employers rank motivation and self-awareness as core skills (WEF Future of Jobs 2025).
- Self-aware consultants stay calm, take feedback, handle ambiguity and adapt to scope changes.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize Notice → Check → Adapt.
