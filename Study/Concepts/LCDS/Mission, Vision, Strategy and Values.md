# Mission, Vision, Strategy and Values
**In one line:** The building blocks of a business: why it exists (mission), where it's going (vision), how it'll get there (strategy), the concrete steps (tactics), and what guides its behavior (core values).

**In plain words:** Every organization should know why it exists, what it hopes to achieve, and how it'll get there. Data scientists need this map because their work has to tie back to it. Most data science lives at the tactics level (campaigns, A/B tests, dashboards), but the insights only matter if they move the strategy and vision forward.

**Terms**
| Block | Means | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Mission | why the company exists | Google: "Organize the world's information and make it universally accessible and useful" |
| Vision | the future end-result it aims for (a goal, not a plan) | Microsoft: "Help people and businesses realize potential" |
| Strategy | high-level, long-term plan to reach the vision (markets, differentiators, resources) | Netflix: global reach with local, original content; usually kept internal |
| Tactics | specific, timed, short-term actions | marketing campaigns, A/B tests, dashboards |
| Core values | guiding principles, the company's "DNA" and culture | John Burns: "Champions of Change…" |

**Key points**
- Vision ≠ strategy: vision is *where*, strategy is *how*.
- Tactics are where data science directly affects outcomes.
- "Your insights must tie back to the bigger picture."

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the five blocks and vision vs strategy.
