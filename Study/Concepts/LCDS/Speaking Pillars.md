# Speaking Pillars
**In one line:** Three habits for every presentation: tell the story, stay focused on the outcome, and stop once you've got it.

**In plain words:** Say what you're going to cover, cover it, then sum up what you covered. Keep steering toward the decision or result you came for. When you've reached it, stop talking: extra words can undo a "yes". Preparation makes all three possible.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Tell them ×3 | preview → content → recap |
| Outcome | the decision or action you want from the audience |
| 6 P's | Proper Planning and Preparation Prevents Poor Performance |

**Key points**
1. **Tell the story:** tell them what you'll tell them, tell them, tell them what you told them.
2. **Focus:** relentlessly on the outcome.
3. **Know when to stop:** once the outcome is reached, stop.
- Read (research), think, then speak. Be concise and precise.
- Delivery: plain words (no jargon or acronyms), vary pitch, slow down, eye contact, good posture, watch the time.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the three pillars.
