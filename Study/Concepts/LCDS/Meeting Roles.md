# Meeting Roles
**In one line:** Six roles that keep a meeting on track: facilitator, timekeeper, note-taker, decision owner, contributors, and subject-matter expert.

**In plain words:** Meetings drift when nobody is clearly in charge of each job. Name who runs the agenda, who watches the clock, who records decisions, and who makes the final call. Contributors come prepared, and experts join only for their item.

**Terms**
| Role | Does |
|---|---|
| Facilitator | runs the agenda, opens and closes, keeps on topic, makes sure every voice is heard |
| Timekeeper | tracks time against the agenda; prompts "decide or defer" |
| Note-taker | captures decisions, actions, owners and deadlines (not a transcript); sends minutes |
| Decision owner | frames options, makes the call if the group can't, accountable afterwards |
| Contributors | read pre-reads, argue from evidence, own their action items |
| Subject-matter expert | joins for one agenda item, briefs, then leaves |

**Key points**
- Assign roles *before* the meeting (part of pre-meeting planning).
- Splitting facilitator and decision owner keeps the discussion fair.
- The note-taker's output drives follow-through.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the six roles.
