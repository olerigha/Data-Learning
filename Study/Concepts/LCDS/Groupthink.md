# Groupthink
**In one line:** A team rushes to agreement to avoid conflict or time pressure, and makes a worse decision. Choose how to decide (consultative, democratic, consensus) on purpose.

**In plain words:** Team decisions are often so informal nobody remembers making them. Under time pressure or stress, teams grab the first plausible idea, defer to the leader, and stop questioning: "Go Along, Get Along". Avoid it by naming the decision method up front. Faster, more top-down methods save time but lose quality and buy-in. Slower, consensus methods cost time but get commitment, which can save time when it's carried out.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| Groupthink | premature agreement that skips critical analysis |
| GAGA | Go Along / Get Along |
| Consultative | one person decides after gathering input |
| Democratic | vote: majority or minority rule |
| Consensus / unanimity | everyone can support it / everyone fully agrees |

**Key points**
- **Causes:** lack of healthy conflict, conformity with the leader, ignoring outside input, time pressure.
- **Eight options**, from fast to slow: one-way pronouncement → human filing cabinet → process info with individuals → with the team → minority rule → majority rule → consensus → unanimity. Moving right: higher quality, more acceptance, more time.
- Urgent ⇒ autocratic or majority vote. Less urgent ⇒ consensus (you can always fall back to a vote).
- Expediency can cost commitment; selling an autocratic decision can take longer than reaching consensus.

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the causes and the quality / acceptance / time trade-off.
