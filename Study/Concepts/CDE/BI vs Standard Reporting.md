# BI vs Standard Reporting
**In one line:** Business intelligence turns data into decisions people can explore themselves; standard reporting just tells you what already happened.

**In plain words:** A standard report is a fixed table emailed every Monday. BI lets the right people slice the data by any dimension, see trends and forecasts, and get fresh numbers, usually on top of a dimensional warehouse. It only works if the underlying data is reliable and the metrics are defined the same way for everyone.

**Terms**
| Term | Means |
|---|---|
| BI | Business Intelligence: reporting, dashboards, OLAP, ad-hoc queries, data discovery |
| Self-service analytics | business users explore data without waiting for IT |
| Certified dataset | governed, tested data product analysts can trust |
| Decision support | BI aimed at specific business goals |

**Key points**
- BI goal: right information, right people, right format, right time.
- BI vs reporting: proactive vs retroactive, multi-dimensional, forecasting, near-real-time.
- Questions before building: business problem? ROI? acceptance criteria? audience? dimensions? KPIs? time periods? SLAs? security?
- BI projects run Agile (Scrum, Kanban), tracked in JIRA or GitHub Projects. See LCDS [[Scrum]] and [[Data Science PM Methodologies]].

**Memorize vs. look up:** Memorize the BI goal line and the pre-build questions.
