Classic Models Dashboard - Answers

Total sales: $9,604,191 (2,996 order lines)

1. Which three countries have the highest sales?
   
USA ($3,273,280, 34.1%), Spain ($1,099,389, 11.4%), France ($1,007,374, 10.5%).
   
What it means: The US alone brings in about a third of revenue and the top three bring in 56%, so the business depends heavily on a few markets. The US relationship is critical, and markets outside the top three are room to grow.



2. Which vendor has the highest sales, and does that change if you filter to Classic Cars?
   
Classic Metal Creations ($934,554). No, it is still #1 when filtered to Classic Cars ($702,577), followed by Gearbox Collectibles ($539,935). Unimax Art Galleries, #2 overall, drops out of the top three.

What it means: About 75% of Classic Metal Creations' sales come from Classic Cars, so it is our most important supplier to protect. It is also a concentration risk if that line slows down.



3. Which product line has the highest sales, and what notable pattern do you see in its trend over time?

Classic Cars ($3,853,922, 40.1% of sales), about double the next line, Vintage Cars ($1,797,560).

Pattern: Sales spike every October-November, reaching about $404K in Nov 2014 and $416K in Nov 2016, roughly 2-3x a normal month. All product lines follow the same seasonal shape. 2015 only has data for Jan-May, so the flat line through late 2015 is a gap in the data, not a drop in sales.
   
What it means: Demand peaks before the holidays, so Classic Cars inventory and marketing should ramp up by early fall.
